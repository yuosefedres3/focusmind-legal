FocusMind — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026

FocusMind ("the app") is a digital-wellbeing app that helps you understand and reduce your phone usage. It is built around one principle: your data never leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. FocusMind has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

Data stored on your device

To do its job, the app stores the following locally on your device only, in the app's private storage:

  • Your focus sessions, goals, streaks, and achievements
  • The app limits and reminder messages you configure
  • Screen-time statistics provided by your device's operating system
  • Your preferences (name, language, theme, notification setting)

This information is never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data in system settings) permanently deletes all of it.

Permissions the app uses

Android

  • Usage access — used to read your screen-time statistics from the system, so the app can show your real usage. This data is processed and stored on-device only.
  • Accessibility Service — used solely to detect which app is in the foreground so FocusMind can show its block screen when an app you limited goes over its daily limit. It is not used to read screen content, capture input, or collect any data, and nothing it observes ever leaves your device.
  • Notifications — used to show optional reminders you enable.
  • Query installed apps — used to list your apps locally so you can pick which ones to limit.

iOS

  • Screen Time (Family Controls) — used so you can choose apps to block during focus sessions. Apple's framework enforces the blocking; Apple never exposes your usage data to us, and we receive nothing.

Purchases

FocusMind Premium is an optional subscription processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Your Premium status is recorded on your device only.

Data export

The app can export your data as a file, but only when you explicitly choose to, and the file goes only where you decide to share it. We never receive a copy.

Children

FocusMind is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Because the app collects no data, material changes are unlikely.
